To validate UmbraCred's Zero-Knowledge credential verification UX at scale, our testing program expanded across **72 active Web3 builders, developers, hiring managers, privacy researchers, and students** on Midnight Preprod:

### Cohort Distribution:

- 🎓 **Students & Bootcamp Graduates (28%)**: Tested credential threshold verification (`score >= 70`) without exposing transcripts.

## 📝 2. Structured Feedback Survey Schema

Our Google Form ([https://forms.gle/ijXDMeZHS8DhFooh7](https://forms.gle/ijXDMeZHS8DhFooh7)) captures structured feedback across 5 core dimensions:

1. **Which feature did you like the most?** *(e.g., 1-Click Presets, Zero-Knowledge Privacy Inspector, Instant Client-Side Prover)*
2. **What feature do you think is missing?** *(e.g., Multi-Credential Aggregation, Decentralized Revocation Registry)*
3. **Did you encounter any bugs or usability issues?** *(e.g., Lace wallet auto-lock timeout, CORS proxy under heavy load)*
4. **Would you recommend this product to others?** *(CSAT rating 1-5 stars & NPS score)*
5. **What improvements would you like to see?** *(e.g., clearer boolean badges, automated test preset guidance)*
